2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (for example levels, in-game currency, items, settings and purchases) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling an App or clearing its data deletes it.

4. Ads and your choices

The Apps are free and show ads. If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), an App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later. On any Android device you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Security & privacy › Privacy › Ads). Where an App offers an optional "No Ads" purchase, buying it removes interstitial and banner ads in that App.

5. How long information is kept

Information collected by the services above is kept according to each provider's retention policies (for example, Google Analytics event data is kept for a limited period set in our project settings, and Crashlytics crash reports are kept for 90 days).

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is processed by the service providers listed above on our behalf, or as described in their policies, and may be disclosed if required by law.

7. Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through an App, contact us and we will help delete it.

8. Your rights and deleting data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because the Apps have no accounts, the simplest way to remove the data on your device is to uninstall the App. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data collected from your device, email us and tell us which App you use. This data is not linked to your name, so we may ask for details that help us find it (such as your device model and the date you played).

9. Security

Information sent by the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when an App is added or starts using a different service. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
